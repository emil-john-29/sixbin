2. The short version

SixBin works without an account. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics tools. We do not collect personal data to identify you or analyse your behaviour.

3. Data on your device

Your favourites, tracker progress, console choice, votes and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

4. Loading content

To keep cheats, tips and the database up to date, the app downloads a content file when it starts and regularly while in use, including in the background. The file is provided via: GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (Dienst „GitHub Pages“).

For technical reasons, the following data is processed: your IP address, date and time of the request, the requested file and technical details of your device (operating system, app version). This data is only needed to deliver the content. We do not receive access logs ourselves and do not combine this data with other data. According to GitHub, it stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. For details, see GitHub's privacy statement (docs.github.com, “GitHub General Privacy Statement”).

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is providing up-to-date content reliably and securely. GitHub is based in the USA. Transfers are based on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. Notifications, widget and sharing

Notifications (launch reminder and alerts about new entries) are created locally on your device. No third-party push services are used and no device identifiers are sent to us. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

The home screen widget only reads content from storage shared between the app and the widget on your device.

When you share an entry, the app creates an image on your device. You decide where to send it via the iOS share sheet.

6. App Store

When you download the app, Apple processes data such as your Apple ID under its own responsibility. Apple's privacy policy applies.

7. No sharing of data

We do not sell data and do not share data with third parties, except with GitHub as named above for delivering content.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21). Just send us an email.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

9. Changes

If the app changes, for example through new features, we will update this policy.
